## Project Roles, Responsibles & Initiation

A **Statement Of Work (SOW)** is a document that includes the description of a project's work requirement. It defines project-specific activities, deliverables, and timelines for a supplier/integrator providing services to the customer. 

The SOW typically addresses the following:
- Executive summary
- Scope of work
- Approach and methodology
- Engagement
- Training
- Assumptions
- Deliverables schedule
- Resource staffing
- Change requests

A **project charter** is a document that describes your project in its entirety. The SOW and the order form would come first, then the Project Charter would draw on information from many sources, including the Business Case. 

The project charter can be divided into three sections:
- Scope
- Objectives
- Deliverables

Other key factors include **budget**, **schedule**, **constraints**, **risks**

### Business Roles
- **Project Manager**: Point of contact for project, tracks status and communication, also an escalation point 
- **Business Process Lead**: Oversees and consolidates KPI's/process
- **Business Sponsor**: Business context, strategy and governance lead
- **Program Manager**: Tracks objectives, business impact and overall goals
- **Engagement Manager**: Accountable for implementation delivery and engagement 
- **Process Owner**: Subject matter expert, defines objectives KPI's and goals, reviews and approves changes
### Platform Roles
- **Platform Owner**: Responsible to maximise value of platform
- **Platform Architect**: SME for guidance, design, technical governance and policies
- **Security Architect**: Reviews security measures and implements enchantments, regular system testing, monitors network security
- **System Administrator**: Maintains platform usability and stability, application maintenance, performance monitoring, error identification/remediation, access control 
- **Developer**: Configures apps and services, delivers new functionality 
### Supporting Roles
- **Business Process Analyst**: Workshop lead, process consultant 
- **Test Manager**: Coordinates test plans
- **Tester**: Creates test cases from requirements, collaborates to validate test cases, test outcomes 
- **Change Enablement Lead**: Designs and implementation strategies to maximise adoption and value 
- **Training Lead**: Responsible for all aspects of training users
- **Communications Lead**: In charge of strategy to communicate all aspects of project to its relevant
