### Machine learning:  
This is often the foundation for an Al system, and is the way we "teach" a computer model to make predictions and draw conclusions from data.    
Deep learning uses layers of algorithms (artificial neural network) to train itself and produce more complex results.  
Generative Al models can produce new content based on what is described in the input.
### Anomaly detection:
The capability to automatically detect errors or unusual activity in a system.    
### Computer Vision:
The capability of software to interpret the world visually through cameras, videos, and images. 
- Image Classification
- Object Detections
- Semantic Segmentation
- Image Analysis
- Face detection, analysis, and recognition
- Optical character recognition
### Natural language processing 
The capability for a computer to interpret written or spoken language, and respond an in kind.
- Analyse and interpret media files
- Interpret spoken language and synthesize speech responses
- Translate spoken and written phrases between languages
- Interpret commands and determine appropriate actions
### Cognitive Computing
Cognitive computing systems differ from conventional computing system in that they can:
- Read and interpret unstructured data
- Understand meaning/intent/context of the way words are used
- Reasons problem in a human-like way and make decisions
- Learn from interactions over time and improve responses 
### Knowledge mining 
The capability to extract information from large volumes of often unstructured data to create searchable knowledge store.  
### Challenge or Risks:
- Bias can affect results: A loan-approval model discriminates by gender due to bias in the data with which it was trained
- Errors may cause harm: An autonomous vehicle experiences a system failure and causes a collision
- Data could be exposed: A medical diagnostic bot is trained using sensitive patients data, which is stored insecurely
- Solutions may not work for everyone: A home automation assistant providing no audio output for visually impaired users 
- Users must trust a complex system: An AI-based financial tool makes investment recommendation - what are they based on?
- Who's liable for AI-driven decisions?: An innocent person is convicted of a crime based on evidence based from facial recognition - who's responsible?
### Responsible Al:    
Usage of Azure OpenAl should follow the six Microsoft Al principles: (PARTIF)  
  
- Privacy and Security: Secure and respect privacy
- Accountability: Created within principal framework to meet legal and ethical
- Reliability and Safety: Avoid any substantial risk to human life
- Transparency: Understand purpose and limitations
- Inclusiveness: Empower and engage people
- Fairness: Use of Al should produce outcomes without bias  
### Precision Regulation:    
- Designate a lead Al ethics official
- Different rules for different risks
- Don't hide your Al
- Explain your Al
- Test your Al for bias
