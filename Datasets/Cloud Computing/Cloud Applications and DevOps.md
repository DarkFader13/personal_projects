## Cloud Native Applications

> Applications built or adapted to operate within a cloud environment.

**Characteristics**:
- Utilize DevOps methodologies.
- Composed of microservices.
- Packaged in containers for portability.
- Capable of running in any environment.
**Advantages**:
- Facilitates quick iterative cycles for feature creation and updates.
- Enables seamless deployment across different environments.
**Application Modernization**:
- Definition: Process of updating and optimizing applications to leverage new technologies and services.
- Goals:
	- Accelerate digital transformation.
	- Utilize cloud computing.
	- Enhance responsiveness to market dynamics.
	- Enable organizations to take advantage of new technologies and services.

## DevOps

> Organizations aim for swift, reliable, and efficient software delivery in today's digital landscape. Integrating development and operations, is pivotal for this transformation.

**Definition**:
- Collaborative approach between development and operations teams.
- Streamlines software delivery lifecycle.
**Key Aspects**:
- Culture of collaboration, automation, and continuous feedback.
- Tools, practices, and processes.
- Aim to reduce overhead, duplication, and rework.
- Addresses complexities of cloud environments.
- Ensures quick and reliable delivery and updates of solutions.
**Components**:
- Continuous Integration (CI).
- Continuous Delivery (CD).
- Continuous Deployment (CDep).
- Continuous Monitoring (CM).
**Tools**:
- Git, Subversion, Jenkins, Bamboo, Prometheus, ELK Stack.

**Benefits of DevOps on Cloud Platforms**:

- Scalability and Flexibility: Cloud resources enable dynamic scaling and workload accommodation.
- Rapid Provisioning and Deployment: Pre-configured environments and automated pipelines reduce time-to-market.
- Cost Optimization: Pay-as-you-go model eliminates upfront investments and reduces operational costs.
- Collaboration and Team Efficiency: Centralized repositories and collaboration tools enhance team collaboration.
- Continuous Integration and Delivery: Integration with DevOps tools streamlines build, test, and deployment processes.

**Conclusion**:
- Impact of DevOps on Cloud: Streamlined software delivery, enhanced collaboration, and scalable resources.
- Adoption on Major Cloud Providers: Accelerates software delivery cycles and adapts to changing business needs.
