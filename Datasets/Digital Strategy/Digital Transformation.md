## Harnessing Crowdsourcing for Innovation

**1. Contests and Competitions:**
- Utilize diversity of opinions and approaches.
- Clear rules and prizes engage crowds in innovation.
- Example: Netflix's $1 million prize for improving its algorithm, attracting over 51,000 contestants.

**2. Communities and Collaboration:**
- Tap into cumulative knowledge and diverse inputs.
- Informal communities foster collaborative problem-solving.
- Contributions range from idea mixing to co-production with modular tasks.

**3. Innovation Funnel Dynamics:**
- Idea generation phase widens the funnel with diverse ideas.
- Crowdsourcing enriches idea pool, enhancing quality.
- Extreme diversity at the start enhances the ultimate output's quality.

**4. Value of Crowdsourcing:**
- Crowdsourcing enables discovery of extreme value ideas.
- Diversity of participation leads to high-quality ideas.
- Firms benefit from outliers and expanded horizons facilitated by crowdsourcing.
